🌿 Food Ayka — Delete Your Account

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time.

Option 1: In the App

1. Open Food Ayka

2. Go to the Profile tab

3. Tap "Reset Profile"

4. Confirm deletion

This immediately removes all your data: profile, scan history, dietary preferences, and signs you out.

Option 2: Email Request

Send an email to hello@getayka.com with subject "Delete My Account" and we will delete your Firebase account and all associated data within 7 days.

What gets deleted:

  • Firebase authentication account (email, name)
  • All dietary profiles
  • All scan history
  • All preferences and settings

What we don't store:

  • Photos (processed on-device only, never uploaded)
  • No server-side scan data exists to delete

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
