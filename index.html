🌿 Food Ayka — Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Introduction

Food Ayka ("we", "our", "the App") is a free food ingredient scanner that analyzes ingredient lists for informational purposes only. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

⚠️ IMPORTANT DISCLAIMER: Food Ayka is an informational tool only and does NOT provide medical, nutritional, or dietary advice. All ingredient assessments, safety scores, allergen detections, and dietary compatibility results are generated automatically by a local database and algorithm. They may contain errors, omissions, or inaccuracies. We accept NO responsibility or liability whatsoever for any decisions made based on the App's results, including but not limited to: allergic reactions, dietary violations, health issues, religious dietary breaches, pet illness or injury, or any other harm of any kind. Always read the actual product label carefully and consult a qualified healthcare professional, nutritionist, allergist, religious authority, or veterinarian before making dietary decisions. You use this App entirely at your own risk.

2. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and display name via Firebase Authentication.

Dietary Profiles: Diet type, allergies, religious preferences, and pet profiles are stored locally on your device only.

Scan History: Stored locally on your device. We do not upload ingredient photos or scan results to any server.

Camera & Photos: Used solely to capture ingredient labels for on-device text recognition. Photos are processed locally and are never uploaded.

3. How We Process Data

All ingredient analysis happens entirely on your device using:

  • Google ML Kit — on-device text recognition (OCR)
  • Local ingredient database — 340+ E-codes, EFSA risk data, NOVA classification
  • Open Food Facts API — ingredient normalization (no personal data sent)

No photos, scan results, or dietary profiles are sent to our servers or any third-party AI service.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase: Authentication. Privacy Policy
  • Google ML Kit: On-device OCR. No data leaves your device.
  • Open Food Facts: Ingredient database. Privacy Policy
  • Telegram: Community features. Privacy Policy

5. Data Storage & Sharing

All personal data is stored locally on your device. We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information. We do not display advertisements or use tracking.
